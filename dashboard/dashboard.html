The 3Pi+ sensors are not in a straight bar: DN2–DN4 sit close together at the front, and DN1/DN5 sit further out and further back. The defaults were measured from a photo of the underside (±2 mm), so check them with a ruler. The axle centre is midway between the wheel centres. Footprint is the width each reading is spread over.
